Data dictionary

--------------------------------------------------------------------
contracts.csv
--------------------------------------------------------------------
contractid          unique identifier of the contract
sales_person_id     unique ID of the person who sold the contract
sales_month         last day of the month the contract was registered
region              region in which the customer lives
customer_gender     customer gender (if given)
household_size      number of people in the customer's household (if given)
occupation          occupation of the customer (if given)
contract_type       CASH (all paid upfront) or FINANCED (PayGo sale)
product             the name of the SKU
price_usd           total price in USD, including the deposit
payment_frequency   for FINANCED contracts, how often the customer is
                    required to pay. NONE for CASH.
perc_deposit        for CASH this is 1. For FINANCED it is the deposit
                    required, expressed as a percentage of total value.
daily_amount_usd    amount the customer is required to pay each day in USD.
                    Even where they are not required to pay daily, this
                    value is expressed as the amount needed per DAY.
tenor_length        number of DAYS the customer must pay to fully pay off
                    the contract. Expressed in days even where the payment
                    frequency is weekly or monthly.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
payments.csv
--------------------------------------------------------------------
contract_id         unique identifier of the contract
pay_month           last day of the month the payment was made in
total_paid          total amount paid in that month, in USD

--------------------------------------------------------------------
calls.csv
--------------------------------------------------------------------
contractid          unique identifier of the contract
call_date           date the customer called
call_reason         reason for the call

--------------------------------------------------------------------
service_tickets.csv
--------------------------------------------------------------------
contractid          unique identifier of the contract
ticket_date         the date the customer brought the product in
ticket_reason       the reason the customer brought the product in
ticket_outcome      the outcome after the team assessed the product

--------------------------------------------------------------------
collections_outreach.csv
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Log of customers contacted under the two regional collections pilots
that ran from April 2026. Regions not listed here ran no pilot.

contractid          unique identifier of the contract contacted
contact_month       last day of the month the contact was attempted
region              region running the pilot
channel             SMS or CALL
attempts            number of contact attempts made that month
reached             whether the customer was actually reached
cost_usd            cost of that month's contact attempts, in USD
